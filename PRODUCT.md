# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SST students and faculty mentors evaluating the RoboSummit robotics competition. They arrive from a shared link or hallway QR code, mostly on phones, with under a minute of attention, deciding whether the event is worth joining.

## Product Purpose

The information hub for SST RoboSummit 2026, season "Planetary Pioneers". A visitor should leave knowing what the competition is, when it happens, what the challenges and rules are, and how to enter. Success is a student answering "what is this, when is it, can I do it, how do I sign up" without asking anyone. Registration is the funnel's exit, but informing comes first.

## Brand Personality

Bold, competitive, playful-serious. The space-mission voice (crews, missions, zones) stays as flavour in the writing and restrained accents. Three words: bold, clear, mission.

## Anti-references

- The previous version of this site: HUD clutter, particle canvases, decorative code labels, corner brackets, mono-type everywhere, panels inside panels. Decoration that carries no information goes.
- Generic dark-mode SaaS: gradient text, identical icon-card grids, hero-metric templates.

## Design Principles

1. Information over decoration: every element on screen reads as a fact or an action, or it does not exist.
2. One voice at a time: the mission theme lives in copy and restrained accents, not in visual machinery.
3. Balanced emphasis: event date, challenges, and registration are all equally findable; nothing buried, nothing shouting alone.
4. Phone-legible in 30 seconds: hierarchy comes from type scale and spacing, not boxes and borders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1. Respect prefers-reduced-motion with instant or crossfade alternatives. Content must remain readable when animation never fires.
